How Do MLC Students Compare on a Global Learning Test?

A simple summary of our PISA-based research, explaining what we found and what it means.

The Big Picture

What We Wanted to Know

MLC follows a project-based learning approach, very different from the more traditional MYP programme. We wanted to understand: how do MLC students perform compared to MYP students on a well-known, globally trusted test? To find out, we administered a PISA-designed diagnostic assessment to a group of MLC and MYP students, and compared how they performed across three subject areas.

🌍 What is PISA? PISA is an internationally recognised test that checks whether students can apply what they've learned to real-life situations — not just memorise facts. It's used by schools and governments around the world to understand how well students think, reason, and solve problems.

✅

MLC students performed about as well as MYP students overall.

Across Science and English, the two groups scored very similarly. In Mathematics, MYP students scored somewhat higher — a difference we are looking into further. Overall, this shows that MLC's project-based approach is helping students build strong, real-world thinking skills.

Looking Ahead

What This Means for MLC

These results are reassuring: even without following a traditional, subject-by-subject curriculum, MLC students are building real-world thinking skills on par with their MYP peers. This shows that learning through projects — exploring real questions, working independently, and creating real outcomes — is helping students grow in ways that matter.

At the same time, MLC isn't just preparing students for tests like PISA. It is intentionally designed to build a wider set of skills that go beyond what any single test can measure.

MLC's Broader Goals for Every Student

Beyond academic performance, MLC continues to focus on developing the whole learner — skills that prepare students not just for exams, but for life.
